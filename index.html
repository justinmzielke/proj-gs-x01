About the work

Sophist captures the tension between permanence and process. Its roughly hewn surface—marked by gestural strokes and raw texture—echoes the artist's stop-motion practice, where beauty emerges through accumulated fragments. Here, that perpetual becoming is frozen in ceramic: a figure not fully arrived, yet undeniably present. The deep brown-black glaze lends the work a quiet gravity, as if the form itself holds the weight of its own becoming.
